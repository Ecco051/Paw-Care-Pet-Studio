Severe Matting Surcharge

Extra hourly surcharge applies for severely matted or difficult coats. Manual quote provided on arrival.

No-Show Fee

50% of the total booking value will be charged for no-shows or cancellations with less than 24 hours notice.

Vaccination Required

Up-to-date vaccination certificate must be provided at check-in for the safety of all pets in our salon.

One Pet Per Booking

One groomer per time slot, one pet per booking. This ensures your fur baby gets our full, undivided attention.

Our Story

Grooming with Love, Since 2014

PawCare Pet Studio was born from a simple belief — every pet deserves to feel safe, loved, and absolutely fabulous. What started as a home-based grooming service in a Sydney garage has grown into the Inner West's most trusted pet salon.

Our certified groomers bring years of experience and a genuine passion for animals. We use only premium, Australian-made, cruelty-free products, and we take the time to understand each pet's unique personality and needs.

From tiny teacup poodles to gentle giant mastiffs, nervous first-timers to seasoned spa regulars — every furry visitor leaves looking their best and feeling loved.
